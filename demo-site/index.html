1. Online fee payment

This page lets parents and guardians pay school fees and transport fees of a student of Metas Adventist School, Ranchi, by entering the student's admission number. The amount shown is the balance due as per the school's records on that day.

2. Transaction charges

Charges levied by the payment gateway or the bank are payable by the payer and are shown before the payment is confirmed. Kindly check the final transaction amount before making the payment.

3. Receipt

A fee receipt is generated on successful payment and, where an email address was given, a PDF copy is emailed. The receipt is the school's acknowledgement of the payment. Please keep it for your records.

4. Accuracy of information

It is the payer's responsibility to enter the correct admission number and to verify the student's name and class shown before paying. The school is not responsible for payments made against a wrong admission number.

5. Contact

For any query regarding fees, please contact the school office at +91 7050555223 or metasschoolranchi@gmail.com.

1. Fees once paid

School fees and transport fees once paid are not refundable, except in the cases below.

2. Duplicate payment

If the same fee is paid twice, the excess amount is refunded to the original payment method, or adjusted against the next fee instalment at the parent's choice.

3. Failed transaction

If the amount is debited from your account but no receipt is generated, the payment is treated as failed. The amount is normally reversed by the bank within 7 to 10 working days. If it is not, please contact the school office with the transaction details.

4. How to request a refund

Write to metasschoolranchi@gmail.com with the student's admission number, the receipt number (if any), the date of payment and the amount. Approved refunds are processed within 10 working days.
